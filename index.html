💌 Festival Greeting Prompts

Sirf "Happy Festival" nahi — emotion, relevance, aur brand connection ke saath greeting banana sikho.

8 Prompts
🎓
Trainer Insight — The Greeting Formula That Works

Festival Ka Rang + Aapki Business Ki Baat = Yaadgaar Message. Ek saree retailer ki Diwali greeting mein rangoli ke design mention hone chahiye. Ek CA firm ki greeting mein "new financial year fresh start" connect hona chahiye. Ye prompts isi philosophy pe based hain.

🪔

01. Diwali — Industry-Connected Greeting

Festival ko aapki business value se naturally connect karo.

COPY-READY PROMPT
Act as an experienced Indian brand strategist who has worked with SMEs across India. Create 3 Diwali greeting concepts for [COMPANY], a [INDUSTRY] business serving [AUDIENCE] in [CITY/REGION]. Our real strength: [REAL BENEFIT] Language: [LANGUAGE] Brand style: [BRAND PERSONALITY] CONCEPT 1 — EMOTIONAL CONNECTION: Connect the Diwali theme of light/new beginnings to a genuine customer transformation your business creates. Give: Headline (max 7 words), 2-line greeting, 1 soft brand line, visual idea. CONCEPT 2 — INDUSTRY METAPHOR: Pick one specific Diwali element (diya, rangoli, crackers, mithai, new clothes, lakshmi puja) and connect it to your actual product or service in a clever, non-forced way. Give: Headline, greeting, why this metaphor works for this specific business. CONCEPT 3 — CUSTOMER-CENTRIC: Write from the customer's perspective — what does Diwali mean to your specific customer type? How does your business make their Diwali better or easier? Give: Headline, greeting, WhatsApp-ready 40-word caption. Rules: No generic "warm wishes and prosperity" language. No fake claims. No forced sales pitch inside the greeting. Keep the festival the hero.
🎨

02. Bilingual Greeting — Hindi + English Naturally

Natural Hinglish jaise dil se nikli ho — AI translation jaisi nahi.

COPY-READY PROMPT
Write a [FESTIVAL] greeting for [COMPANY], a [INDUSTRY] business, in THREE natural versions: VERSION A — ENGLISH: Professional, warm, with subtle brand recall. VERSION B — HINDI: Pure Devanagari, reads like a real Indian business message, uses natural Hindi idioms. NOT a literal translation. VERSION C — HINGLISH: The way educated Indian businesspeople actually speak — flows naturally between both languages. Include a festival phrase in Hindi, main message in Hinglish. For each version provide: - Visual headline (max 6 words) - Main greeting (max 20 words) - Optional brand line (max 10 words) - WhatsApp-ready caption (max 35 words) - Which audience this version suits best Audience: [AUDIENCE] Subtle value to reinforce: [REAL BENEFIT] City/region context: [CITY] IMPORTANT: Do not simply translate. Make each version feel native to people who speak that language naturally. A Gujarati businessman in Surat reads differently than a Delhi corporate buyer.
🤝

03. Dealer & B2B Partner Greeting

Aapke channel partners ko special feel karwao — customers se alag message.

COPY-READY PROMPT
Write a [FESTIVAL] message from [COMPANY] to our dealer/distributor network and B2B partners. Our business: [INDUSTRY], Products: [PRODUCTS] What dealers value about us: [REAL BENEFIT] Number of dealer partners (approx): [NUMBER or "a strong network across India"] This is NOT a customer greeting. Dealers and channel partners are growth partners. Acknowledge: 1. The business we've built TOGETHER — shared wins, orders fulfilled, trust built. 2. What the coming season means for business (realistic, not overselling). 3. A genuine thank-you that feels personal, not corporate. Deliver: - Short formal letter version (for email, max 120 words) - WhatsApp message version (max 50 words, warm and direct) - One-line poster caption for a dealer appreciation visual - A subject line for the email Tone: Partner-to-partner, not supplier-to-buyer. Respectful, direct, genuine.

PRIMARY GREETING — Celebrates Eid genuinely and respectfully. Connects to values of togetherness, gratitude, and new beginnings — which are also core to our business. NOT forced commercial. 2. UNIVERSAL VERSION — For our mixed customer base; acknowledges the occasion with warmth without assuming religion. Suitable for all community members. 3. B2B PARTNER VERSION — For business partners who are celebrating Eid; acknowledges the holiday in a partner-to-partner way. 4. SOCIAL MEDIA CAPTION — 30 words, suitable for Instagram/Facebook/LinkedIn. Cultural guidelines to follow: - Use "Eid Mubarak" correctly (not "Happy Eid" as primary greeting) - Crescent moon / star symbolism is appropriate - Avoid food references (fasting context) - Reference of brotherhood, gratitude, charity are appropriate - Do NOT turn Eid into a discount announcement

07. Regional Festival Greeting (Pongal / Onam / Baisakhi / Bihu)

Regional festivals pe accurate cultural connect karo — generic mat bano.

COPY-READY PROMPT
Create a [REGIONAL FESTIVAL — e.g. Pongal / Onam / Baisakhi / Bihu / Ugadi] greeting for [COMPANY], a [INDUSTRY] business. Our customers in this region: [DESCRIBE — e.g. Tamil Nadu saree retailers, Kerala hotel owners, Punjab farmers and agri-dealers] Products/Services: [PRODUCTS] Language for this region: [REGIONAL LANGUAGE + English version] Research task for AI: Identify 3-5 AUTHENTIC cultural elements of this specific festival (not generic India-wide symbols). Examples: - Pongal: Kolam (rangoli), sugarcane, newly harvested rice, Pongal pot - Onam: Pookalam (flower carpet), Onasadya feast, boat race, Maveli story - Baisakhi: Harvest of wheat, bhangra, new agricultural year, Gurdwara visit - Bihu: Xorai offering, Bihu dance, gamosa (red and white cloth), Brahmaputra river Now connect ONE of these authentic elements to our actual business in a genuine, clever way — not forced. Create: Regional-language greeting, English translation, poster concept, and WhatsApp caption (35 words). Cultural respect rule: The festival symbol must connect naturally. If the connection feels forced, choose a different element.

08. Business Anniversary + Festival Combo

Jab aapka business anniversary festival ke paas ho — double impact create karo.

COPY-READY PROMPT
Create a combined [FESTIVAL] + Business Anniversary message for [COMPANY]. Verified facts only: - Company name: [COMPANY] - Years in business: [VERIFIED YEARS] - Location: [CITY] (and any expansion since founding) - One real milestone: [ONE TRUE ACHIEVEMENT — e.g. "Crossed 1,000 clients", "Expanded to 3 states", "Launched XYZ product"] - What customers say we do best: [REAL CUSTOMER FEEDBACK OR KNOWN STRENGTH] Tasks: 1. Founder's note (120 words max) — reflective, genuine, not boastful. Thank customers and team authentically. 2. Social media post — connects the anniversary to the festival theme (new light = new chapter). 50 words. 3. WhatsApp broadcast message — personal, direct, warm. 40 words. 4. One headline for a poster — max 8 words, combines anniversary joy and festival energy. CRITICAL RULES: - Use ONLY the verified facts provided. No invented client numbers, revenue, awards. - Do not exaggerate years or scale. - Make it feel like a genuine milestone, not a marketing stunt. - The founder should sound human, not like a press release.

12. AI Poster Review & Critic

Bhejne se pehle apna poster AI se review karwao — ek demanding brand director ki tarah.

COPY-READY PROMPT
Review my [FESTIVAL] poster as a demanding Indian brand director who has approved and rejected hundreds of SME campaigns. My business: [COMPANY] — [INDUSTRY] | Audience: [AUDIENCE] [UPLOAD THE POSTER IMAGE] SCORE 1-10 on these criteria (be brutally honest): 1. THUMB-STOP POWER — Would this stop someone scrolling WhatsApp? 2. GREETING AUTHENTICITY — Does it feel genuine or copy-pasted? 3. BRAND RECALL — Will people remember WHO sent this 5 minutes later? 4. CULTURAL ACCURACY — Are the festival elements used correctly and respectfully? 5. INDUSTRY CONNECTION — Does the business feel naturally connected to the festival? 6. MOBILE READABILITY — Can it be read on a Redmi phone screen, in a bright room? 7. LOGO & URL ACCURACY — Are these correct, clear, and not distorted? 8. OVERALL IMPRESSION — Would YOU share this with your clients? After scoring: - List 3 specific things to REMOVE - List 3 specific things to FIX (with exact suggestions) - Write the improved headline and greeting copy - Give an overall verdict: SEND / REVISE / DO NOT SEND — with reason

17. Offer Announcement Reel

Create a festival offer announcement reel for [COMPANY]. 💬 WhatsApp Campaign Prompts

India mein 90% SME business WhatsApp pe hota hai. Ye prompts usi ke liye hain.

Trainer Fact — WhatsApp Reality Check

Indian customers 8-10 festival broadcasts ek din mein receive karte hain. Aapka message unka 7th "Happy Diwali" nahi hona chahiye. Ye prompts use karo aur ek aisa message bhejo jo unhe actually useful lage — ya genuinely touch kare. Tab woh aapka number save karenge, block nahi karenge.

📱 THE WHATSAPP RULE — Always remember:

WhatsApp message mein: First 2 lines sabse important hain — yahi preview mein dikhta hai. Headline pehle, festival wish pehle, company name baad mein. Offer hai toh ₹ amount clearly dikhe. Max 60-80 words for best response. Aur ek clear next step dena — call, visit ya reply karein.

MESSAGE 2 — FOR EXISTING CUSTOMERS you want to cross-sell to: Reference a specific seasonal need (post-festival scenario for your industry). One genuine, relevant offer or value. Max 60 words. Iss step-by-step plan ko follow karo — 20 minute mein ek complete campaign ready.

⏱ Min 1-2
STEP 01
Choose your moment

Festival, recurring day, customer milestone, ya seasonal business opportunity. Skip irrelevant occasions.

⏱ Min 3-5
STEP 02
Fill Campaign Builder

Company, industry, audience, one real benefit, website. Use specific language — not generic descriptions.

⏱ Min 6-10
STEP 03

⏱ Min 11-14
STEP 04

⏱ Min 15-16
STEP 05
Add real branding

Your actual logo, exact website, company name spelling. Don't trust AI to get these right.

⏱ Min 17-18
STEP 06
Write WhatsApp message

Short, specific, festival first — company second. Use WhatsApp prompt from this lab.

⏱ Min 19
STEP 07
Run quality checklist

Check spelling, logo, URL, cultural fit, mobile readability — before hitting send.

⏱ Min 20
STEP 08
Send & track responses

Note real replies, enquiries, and feedback — not just "seen" counts. Meaningful responses = it worked.

🎓 Trainer's 3 Golden Rules:
1. Festival first, company second. The occasion earns attention; the brand earns recall. Don't reverse this.
2. Real beats perfect. An authentic behind-the-scenes photo beats a generic stock Diwali graphic every time.
3. Useful beats promotional. One genuinely useful tip during festival season is worth 10 "Happy Diwali" broadcasts.
